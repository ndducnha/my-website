Biography

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science (Cybersecurity) at VinUniversity and Head of the Southern Regional Representative Office, concurrently Head of Innovation, at the National Center for Barcode and Traceability (NCBT). I am also the Founder of VCyber Lab at VinUniversity, the Cybersecurity & AI Applications Research Lab. On the industry side I serve as Senior Advisor at VCyber Technology, a software company developing enterprise digital-transformation products. Its flagship products include a traceability and electronic label application, OKI secure messaging and OKA secure storage. I received my PhD and completed my postdoctoral research at Deakin University (Australia), and hold a Master's degree from the Queensland University of Technology (QUT).

My research centers on Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), AI for Cybersecurity, IoT Security and Robotics Security. I have published across leading Q1 journals such as IEEE TDSC and IEEE TIFS, together with a range of international publications, research projects, awards and industry collaborations.

Beyond my academic work, I also work across government, academia and industry, particularly in cybersecurity, digital transformation and national digital infrastructure. My work includes supporting regulators and enterprises in implementing Vietnamese technical standards such as QCVN 11 (QCVN 135), QCVN 06, QCVN 134 and QCVN 14, providing technical consulting for regulatory bodies, testing laboratories and camera manufacturers in meeting the required technical criteria.
